Remmo — Support

26 October 2026

Türkçe

Remmo turns your iPhone, iPad and Mac into a remote for Android TV and Google TV. Nothing needs to be installed on the TV.

Getting started

  1. Make sure your TV is on and connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your device.
  2. Open Remmo and allow Local Network access when asked.
  3. Choose your TV from the list and type the code shown on the TV screen.

Supported TVs

TVs and streaming devices running Android TV or Google TV, including Sony BRAVIA, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Xiaomi Mi Box, NVIDIA SHIELD and Chromecast with Google TV.

My TV doesn’t appear

  • Check that the TV and your device are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network).
  • Turn on Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › Remmo.
  • Some routers block device discovery. Use Add by IP Address and enter the IP shown on the TV under Settings › Network.

“Not paired” or “TV identity changed”

If you reset the TV or it was updated, pair it again from the TV menu at the top of the remote. “TV identity changed” means the TV answered with a different security key than when you paired it; if you didn’t reset the TV, check that you’re on your own network.

Widgets, Control Center and Siri

Add the Remmo widget from your Home Screen’s edit mode, or add TV Power, Mute or Play/Pause to Control Center. These control your default TV, which you can change in Settings. You can also say “Press Power on Remmo” to Siri.

Contact

Something not working? Open an issue at github.com/VS-57/remmo-legal/issues with your TV model and what happened.

Remmo — Destek

Başlarken

  1. TV'nin açık ve cihazınızla aynı Wi-Fi ağında olduğundan emin olun.
  2. Remmo'yu açın ve sorulduğunda Yerel Ağ iznini verin.
  3. Listeden TV'nizi seçin ve TV ekranındaki kodu girin.

TV'm görünmüyor

  • TV ile cihazınızın aynı Wi-Fi'de (misafir ağı değil) olduğunu kontrol edin.
  • Ayarlar › Gizlilik ve Güvenlik › Yerel Ağ › Remmo açık olsun.
  • Bazı modemler cihaz bulmayı engeller. IP Adresiyle Ekle'yi kullanıp TV'nin Ayarlar › Ağ bölümündeki IP adresini girin.
